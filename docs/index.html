Three ways the home page
could feel.

— A short note from the studio —

Each concept hits the same brief — same-day Dubai gifting, premium feel, the four sections you asked for — but pushes a different emotional register. Editorial reads like a quiet magazine; Noir leans into after-dark indulgence; Desert is the warmest, most Gulf-rooted of the three.

Every concept ships with three palette swaps, three type pairings and three hero compositions you can toggle live via the Tweaks panel — so a final direction can be a remix of any two.
