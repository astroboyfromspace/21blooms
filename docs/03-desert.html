— From the atelier —

A small studio
in Al Quoz.

21Bloom is six pairs of hands, a cool room, and a long table. We compose to order, twice daily — Sat through Thu — and we never use a foam base or a plastic sleeve.

Stems are flown in overnight from fourteen growers — Holland, Kenya, Italy, and a small farm just outside Sharjah. What doesn't sell that day is given to the school next door.

— Noor, founder.
